Privacy Policy — DengueWatch BD

Effective 9 September 2026 · Android app

DengueWatch BD does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. What you enter, and your location, stay on your phone.

What the app does

DengueWatch BD shows publicly published dengue surveillance figures for Bangladesh from the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), a symptom guide based on WHO dengue warning-sign criteria, a directory of hospitals, and prevention information.

Information stored on your device

The following never leaves your phone. It is not uploaded, and it is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google account.

  • Your chosen area and app settings, such as language and alert preferences.
  • Your symptom log, if you save entries from the symptom check. It is held in the app's private storage on the device.
  • Your location, when you grant permission. It is used only on the phone.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Location

Location is optional. The app works without it — you can choose your area manually.

When granted, location is used on the device for three purposes:

  • To show the dengue risk reading for the area you are actually in.
  • To find hospitals near you, by handing the search to your maps app.
  • To notice when you enter an area in the high or severe risk band, and warn you.

The third purpose requires background location. Android must be able to check your area while the app is closed, or an entry warning could only appear when you already had the app open, which defeats its purpose. You are asked for this separately, only after you switch the warning on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in Android's settings.

Your location is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no server that receives it.

Network connections

The app makes only these requests, and none carry your personal data:

HostPurposeWhat is sent
druzzal.github.io Downloads the public dengue figures A plain request for a public JSON file. No parameters, no body.
tiles.openfreemap.org Map tiles for the risk map Requests for the tiles being displayed.

As with any internet request, these hosts can see your device's IP address and which files were requested, which is inherent to how the internet works. We do not receive that information — there is no server operated by this app.

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics or social media SDKs.

Children

The app is general-purpose public-health information. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Medical disclaimer

DengueWatch BD is an information tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything. The symptom check follows WHO dengue warning-sign criteria to help you decide when to seek care; it is not a substitute for seeing a clinician.

The figures come from DGHS and count patients reported by hospitals, so they undercount anyone who had dengue but never sought hospital care.

DengueWatch BD is an independent app that uses official DGHS data. It is not made by, or endorsed by, DGHS. The underlying data feed is published at druzzal.github.io/dengue-bd-dashboard.
